      The pay band

      Roles at firms that clear a pay band are tagged: £60,000 a year in the UK, and the same multiple of the national median elsewhere, about $95,000 in the US and C$100,000 in Canada. A solid tag means there is evidence: pay stated in a US listing, or published reports of graduate pay at that firm in that country. A dashed tag means likely: a top quant firm, big tech company or AI lab with no firm-specific figure, or a US estimate. Reported figures are often total first-year pay including bonus and stock, and intern pay is pro rata.

      How pay is handled

      Stated pay comes straight from the source listing and is in US dollars. Where a US role states none, a dashed figure shows an estimate: the median of the same employer's stated pay for the same role type. Hourly intern pay is converted to a yearly figure at 2,080 hours. The green edge marks roles above the US median for full-time workers, $64,064 a year (BLS, Q3 2026). The UK median is £40,911 (ONS, April 2026) and Canada's is C$68,640 (Statistics Canada, 2025).

      Degree, season and visa labels

      These come from the source lists and from words in the job title. Many roles carry none, so the filters only narrow by what is stated. Visa notes exist for a few hundred US roles only.

      Where the listings come from

      Combined and de-duplicated from public GitHub lists: SimplifyJobs, vanshb03, speedyapply, zshah101, sndsh404, negarprh, northwesternfintech and LorenzoLaCorte, plus a few roles read from employer career sites. Every title links to the listing itself.
